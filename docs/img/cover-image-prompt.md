# Cover Image Prompt

Please generate a polished, fun cover image for this textbook. The image will
be used as a website cover and social-media preview.

## Required Specifications

- Format: PNG
- Wide landscape format
- Final size: 1200×630 pixels (1.91:1 Open Graph aspect ratio)
- Use case: stylized-concept educational website cover

The image has four layers, back to front: background montage, unifying color
treatment, mascot, and title text.

## Subject and Tone

This is a college-level guide to designing and building intelligent textbooks
with generative AI, learning graphs, interactive MicroSims, Markdown, and open
web publishing. It is for curious students and educators with minimal prior
programming experience. The tone should be optimistic, playful, inventive, and
technically credible—future-of-learning imagery that still feels welcoming.

## Title

Render this exact title once:

**"Intelligent Textbooks"**

Place it in the center in a large, bold, clean geometric sans-serif typeface.
Use warm white lettering with a subtle navy shadow or translucent dark-blue
scrim. Preserve generous quiet space immediately behind the words. The title
must be the clearest element and remain legible at thumbnail size. Spell both
words exactly and add no subtitle or other text.

## Background Montage

Arrange these eight visual concepts around the center title as one integrated
panoramic illustration rather than separate boxes:

1. **Living digital book** — an open book emitting warm light, with pages
   transforming into interactive diagrams and small luminous particles.
2. **Learning graph** — connected concept nodes and directional dependency
   paths forming a graceful constellation.
3. **Generative AI** — an abstract neural network or friendly intelligence
   spark collaborating with the book, without a humanoid robot.
4. **Bloom's Taxonomy** — a subtle ascending six-step learning staircase,
   represented through icons rather than tiny labels.
5. **Interactive MicroSim** — a browser-like card containing a colorful
   hands-on simulation with sliders, a graph, and moving geometric elements.
6. **Code and mathematics** — a few elegant code brackets, a simple equation,
   and chart curves woven into the composition, never dense or tiny.
7. **Open web publishing** — connected pages and an open globe suggesting
   free worldwide access and reusable educational resources.
8. **Human–AI co-creation** — a stylus and cursor shaping lesson content
   together, expressed symbolically without photorealistic human faces.

Keep montage elements strongest near the outer edges and corners. Let them
fade into a calmer central zone so they frame, rather than compete with, the
title.

## Mascot

Use the attached reference image as the identity reference for Axiom, the
book's owl mascot. Place Axiom in the lower-left corner, welcoming the reader
with the same joyful open-wing pose, large round black glasses, blue feathers,
white chest, golden beak and feet, yellow collar, and red necktie. Preserve
the recognizable character design and friendly expression. Show the owl from
head to feet, occupying roughly 22–27% of the canvas height. The mascot may
slightly overlap the montage but must not overlap the title.

## Style and Composition

- Style: richly polished digital illustration with crisp vector-like shapes,
  gentle painterly texture, and subtle dimensional lighting.
- Palette: deep navy and electric blue, bright teal, warm amber/gold, and
  small coral-red accents that harmonize with Axiom.
- Lighting: bright, optimistic, magical glow from the book, with a subtle
  vignette at the edges.
- Composition: wide cinematic panorama; title centered; montage in a loose
  orbit around it; mascot firmly in the lower-left; balanced visual weight on
  the right with learning-graph and MicroSim imagery.
- Finish: professional educational-technology branding, energetic and fun,
  readable and uncluttered at social-preview size.

## Avoid

- No text other than the exact title "Intelligent Textbooks".
- No misspellings, duplicated words, subtitle, labels, logos, or watermark.
- No dense paragraphs, tiny UI copy, or illegible pseudo-text.
- No generic stock-photo clichés, corporate handshakes, humanoid robots, or
  photorealistic faces.
- Do not place montage elements over the title.
- Do not crop the owl, redesign its colors, remove its glasses or necktie, or
  move it away from the lower-left.
- Do not use a grid of disconnected panels; the montage must read as one
  cohesive world.
