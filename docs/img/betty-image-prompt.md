# Betty – Image Prompt

Betty is the Book Manager agent for the Intelligent Textbooks project. She coordinates
every AI skill that has to run before a book is finished, watches the quality of each book,
and keeps the team (Penny, Margo, Hank, and Candy) moving. She loves reading, and she's
passionate about helping children learn.

Her name has three "B" meanings: **B**ook manager, **B**londe, and a nod to the
early-1960s elegance of the Betty Draper character from *Mad Men*. She's an original
character, not a portrait of any real actress.

Her signature visual: **she always has a book in her hands**, usually a bright yellow one
that matches her yellow-book avatar.

## Main Prompt (square portrait, matches Penny and Margo)

```
A warm, photorealistic-illustrated square portrait (1:1) of Betty, an original character
and the elegant, friendly book manager of an intelligent-textbook publishing team. She is a
woman in her mid-30s with honey-blonde hair styled in soft, voluminous early-1960s curls
that fall just above her shoulders. She has bright blue eyes, a kind and confident smile,
classic winged eyeliner, and soft coral-pink lipstick.

Her outfit is modern with a tasteful 1960s twist: a fitted powder-blue cardigan over a
crisp white collared blouse, a thin string of pearls, and small pearl earrings. A small
gold book-shaped brooch is pinned near her collar.

She holds an open bright yellow hardcover book close to her chest with both hands, one
finger keeping her place, as if she just looked up from reading to greet you. Her
expression says she is encouraging, patient, and happy to help.

Setting: a cozy, sunlit library-office. Behind her, softly blurred, are tall wooden
bookshelves full of colorful textbooks, a few children's picture books with the covers
facing out, a small globe, and a corkboard with neatly organized index cards and a printed
checklist with green checkmarks (her publishing workflow). A brass desk lamp glows warmly.
On the desk in the foreground: a neat stack of textbooks, a cup of tea in a mid-century
teacup, a yellow pencil, and an open laptop showing a book's table of contents.

Lighting: warm golden-hour light from a window on the left, soft rim light on her hair,
gentle shallow depth of field. The colors are warm creams, butter yellow, powder blue,
and wood tones.

Style: polished digital illustration close to photorealism, the same rendering style and
quality as the portraits of her teammates Penny (navy blazer, network-graph background)
and Margo (teal blazer, marketing-studio background). Head and upper body, centered, with
eye contact. No text, no logos, no watermark.
```

## A Note on Spelling

Betty is patient with spelling. Dan is dyslexic, and Betty quietly fixes typos and never
makes a fuss about them. Her look should feel warm and supportive, never stern or
"red-pen" strict.

## Things to Avoid

- No likeness of any real actress or person; she's an original character
- No glasses (Penny wears them) and no red hair (that's Margo), so the team looks distinct
- No cigarettes or period vices; she's a role model for kids
- No stern librarian or "shushing" pose
- No text or lettering in the image
